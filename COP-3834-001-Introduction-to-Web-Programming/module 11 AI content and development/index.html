Manifesto

Aerospace engineering is no longer a tool applied at the end of a process, but a condition that structures how humanity expands. We design inside systems defined by physics, orbital mechanics, networks, and structural integrity.

The aim is not to aestheticize technology. The aim is to engage the cosmos as it is structured today—complex, hostile, contingent, and infinite. Complexity is neither avoided nor simplified away. It is engineered and conquered.
