Preserving Earth's Oceans for Future Generations

Our oceans generate over half the world's oxygen, regulate climate, and host millions of species. Yet, they face unprecedented threats from plastic pollution, overfishing, and climate change.

Our Shared Mission

We are dedicated to combating marine destruction through community education, policy advocacy, and hands-on cleanup initiatives.

Zero Plastic Waste

Over 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans annually. We advocate for systemic bans on single-use plastics and fund local beach cleanups globally.

Climate Resilience

Ocean warming and acidification disrupt marine ecosystems. We support policies promoting renewable energy and coral reef restoration.

Protected Areas

Establishing marine sanctuaries allows marine wildlife to recover and thrive. We campaign to protect 30% of global waters by 2030.
